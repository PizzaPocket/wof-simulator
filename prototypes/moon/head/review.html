NightWing skull and jaw volume study

Structural study for review. This is not Moon’s finished model. The skull, jaw connection, seated eyeballs and blinking lids are being fitted. Horns, ears, plating, teeth and expression muscles are not built in this study.

Drag to orbit; scroll to zoom. Head volume is constructed along its local axis. Reference pose tilts the entire assembly afterward. Jaw volume has its own local axis and rigid hinge. The hinge test is not a fitted bite or finished facial rig.

Clay supplies the proportional skull-width envelope. NightWing muzzle taper and the drawn roof, orbit and jaw profiles supply the local differences. The broader skull remains a working hypothesis; the side drawing cannot establish its true width.
