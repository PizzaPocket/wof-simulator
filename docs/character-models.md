# Character model review

Press **F8** or **Y / Triangle** in the game to enter the review room, or run
game/scenes/character_review.tscn directly. It uses the real game actors.

- Drag outside the controls to orbit; scroll to zoom. Q/E or the right stick also orbits.
- Choose **Whole body** or **Face** for framing.
- Dragon poses include resting fold, spread wings, flight, and a plain-clay shape view.
- **Play as** enables normal movement. Humans walk, run and jump.
- **Tab**, D-pad left, or 1–6 changes the reviewed character. F8 returns to the village.
- Local reference images can be shown alongside the model. They are private cached
  reference material, not bundled game art.

## Starflight

Starflight is a NightWing with two era-specific profiles, `starflight` and `starflight_blind`, in
`game/data/protagonists.json`. Both set `"model": "moon"`, so they reuse Moon's baked mesh, rig and
anatomy at a larger inferred scale (1.95) rather than a new Blender build, and are not playable.
Source: the wiki mirror's *Starflight* page.

| Era | Profile | Where | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arc 1 | `starflight` (sighted) | MudWing village, when the map's era is Arc 1 | The wiki places him in the Mud Kingdom in *The Dragonet Prophecy* |
| Arc 2 | `starflight_blind` | Jade Mountain Academy library | Head librarian and co-founder (*Moon Rising*, *Talons of Power*) |

He was blinded and burned in the volcanic eruption in *The Dark Secret*. The blind profile swaps the
seated eye globes for clouded, pupil-less ones. **Not done:** his burn scars (the wiki describes long
scars across his scales and snout), the midnight-black palette with purple and blue glints and silver
wing undersides (the shared model keeps Moon's palette), and any slimmer build. A proper variant would
add build entries for both ids in `game/tools/build_tribe_dragons.py`. The wiki gives no body size,
so the scale is a guess.

## Moon full game pass

The complete asset is game/assets/dragons/moon.glb and is used by the ordinary
Moon village actor, X possession, collected-character switching, and flight.
The separate browser head preview is no longer part of this workflow.

- The broad cranial foundation, NightWing bridge/orbits and fitted jaw join the
  cervical body. A domed posterior cranial vault continues behind the orbital
  rim into the upper cervical attachment. The spinal centerline forms an
  S-curve and its rig stations follow the upper arch, recessed middle bend and
  fuller lower neck, approaching the skull base almost horizontally. The game head build reuses the source without exporting or
  updating the standalone study artifacts. The anterior muzzle tapers into a
  supported tapered premaxilla with a small terminal overhang. A separate fitted
  short nasal shell converges to a narrow terminal beak and has real nostril
  apertures cut through the shell and carrier, with recessed dark cavities. Facial colors follow anatomical parts, not height.
- Continuous skull-grown horn housings cover the buried horn roots. Nearly
  straight, gently rising charcoal keratin shafts have a restrained satin contrast.
  Cupped ears, curved thickness-bearing crown/back blades and paired silver tears
  finish the head. The lateral eye assembly uses Clay's single pigmented globe
  construction: iris and pupil cannot project through fitted lids. Upper lids
  retain three fitted blink in-betweens; build-time rays check full coverage.
- The lower jaw is fitted to the final upper bite, with a continuous chin and
  rounded front arc. A smooth tooth-bearing plane is independent of the lip
  contour; the fitted lip retracts slightly on opening. Weighted cheek tissue
  and a soft rear oral wall connect the back of the mouth. Clay's crown primitive supplies surface-seated curved dental
  arches, including six upper and five lower anterior teeth. The muscular tongue
  follows the actual oral floor with .006 model-unit minimum clearance.
- The shared anatomical prototype supplies named shoulder, arm, forearm, hip,
  thigh and calf muscles. Their attachments and profiles are retargeted to the
  NightWing limb chain and fuller torso. Clay-derived independent dorsal/ventral
  depths, rounded square sections and ventral keel replace the narrow elliptical
  trunk. The broad cervical attachment meets the skull and shoulder girdle.
  Ventral scutes fit the host skin; curved blades continue into the tail. Muscle
  radii retain 96% of the Clay prototype before world scaling. Clay's toe
  organization and hooked claw paths are reused at 90%, with embedded metacarpals and a small raised fifth digit.
- Each wing has a shoulder/humerus, forearm and five two-segment digit chains.
  Clay-derived muscle bellies, elbow anatomy, carpal saddle and finger-root
  knuckles replace bare arm rods. Fold rotations preserve every support
  segment's length. Membranes and silver
  underwing scales share the supports' weights; Moon has no compressed fold morphs.
  The reusable raised_fan pose lifts the wrist above the head/back and fans the
  contracted digits outward and aft. The body-side digit stays innermost and
  the outer flight digit stays farthest out; the fingers gather more closely,
  with the pointer lower than the second ray to form the reference arc.
  Distinct proximal/distal directions make a cupped
  surface rather than a vertically flat fan. Segment lengths remain invariant.
- Walking cadence uses stride length, with a shorter support phase when running.
  All dragons now share Clay's actual gait, gallop/sprint, staged jump, climbing,
  flight-limb, effort-driven flap/glide, gaze, tail and blink components through
  an anatomy profile. See shared-dragon-motion.md for the mapping and checks.
  Fire uses the fitted jaw joint and muzzle nozzle.

Build only Moon with Blender's game/tools/build_tribe_dragons.py followed by
-- moon, then run Godot's editor import. game/tests/moon_game_pass.gd exercises
her actual village actor, X possession, movement, fire, flight, staged lids,
fold stowage and fixed support lengths at five folds. Its rendered captures come
from the village under cache/moon-in-game, not a separate inspection scene.

### Age and inferred physical scale

[Moon's book-cited description](https://mirror.wingsoffire.wiki/wiki/Moonwatcher)
gives age four, small size and powerful muscles. The
[species account](https://mirror.wingsoffire.wiki/wiki/Dragons) cites rapid growth
until roughly seven, then slower continuing growth. This rules out treating a
four-year-old as a human preschooler or using 4/7 as a linear height fraction.
[Starflight](https://mirror.wingsoffire.wiki/wiki/Starflight), age seven, provides
a same-tribe near-maturity comparator, but no verified numeric measurements were
found for either character.

The current **game estimate**, not canon, uses 87% of a near-mature NightWing's
linear scale (plausible design range 75–90%). Geometry currently measures about
8.4 m nose to tail, approximately 2.6 m at the shoulder, 3.8 m to the head excluding
horns and 15.0 m spread wingspan. The game's existing Clay/human scale anchors these
numbers; there is no published dragon-to-meter conversion behind them.

Mass is especially uncertain: closed primary anatomy volume multiplied by an
assumed effective density gives about 4.4 tonnes (3.1–6.5 tonnes with the chosen
450–950 kg/m³ range). This is a speculative modeling estimate, not a book fact
or a physics mass change. Parameters and sources live in moon_design.json;
the exported moon_anatomy.json reports geometry measurements after each build.

## Build and verify

Run Blender with game/tools/build_tribe_dragons.py to build Moon, Blue and the
deferred Darkstalker model. Run game/tools/build_scavengers.py with the
umber_pouch argument for the pouch, and game/tools/build_wren.py with the wren
argument for Wren. Wren additionally needs the pinned female-young anatomy target.
Run Godot's editor import after rebuilding generated assets. Generated GLBs and
downloaded reference art remain ignored.

The new_cast_smoke.gd check covers X possession, Wren's human movement and
capabilities, four SilkWing wings, flight, the closed collision perimeter and
ceiling, and Darkstalker's gameplay exclusion. Existing possession, village and
Clay climbing checks run separately. render_character_review.gd captures bodies,
faces, wings, clay shapes and the small-window interface.

## Current review status

These are editable first sculpts, not approved final likenesses. Facial forms,
scute layout, wing outlines and folded silhouettes still need visual review
against the artwork. Blue's rearward fold is a reconstruction from available
references, not a claim that a definitive folded-wing drawing was found.

The independent rigs support walking, running, jumping, flight, wing spreading,
blinking and neck aim. Moon can breathe fire; Blue cannot. Playable dragons use the shared claw-contact climbing system. Wren cannot fly or breathe fire.

The invisible region perimeter and ceiling use two-sided collision geometry.
Falling recovery remains for accidental falls beneath the terrain.


## Follow-on character pass, 13 November 2026

Darkstalker now shares the corrected NightWing builder, not the older thin-body
NightWing approximation. `darkstalker_design.json` inherits `moon_design.json`
and records character differences explicitly. Full dental arcs, fitted cheek
and tongue, staged lateral eyes, horn housings, segmented digits, muscle profiles
and sampled wing attachment boundaries are inherited. Only Darkstalker is rebuilt
by `build_tribe_dragons.py -- darkstalker`; Moon, Clay and Umber assets stay intact.
He remains excluded from the village and collection. The giant world scale is
independent of the camera framing used by the existing review room.

Blue's next sculpt is documented in `blue_design.json` and
`docs/blue-anatomy-review.md`. This is a reference/anatomy pass, not a claim that
the existing Blue model has already been rebuilt to Moon's level. The official
SilkWing tribe sheet and larger official-art extracts are cached locally.


The subsequent folded-wing attachment correction removes the separate proximal
arm triangle. Both Moon and Darkstalker now trace the back of the actual
shoulder–elbow–wrist chain, with sampled weights that follow its bend. Blue's
anatomy contract includes this constraint before his replacement wing build.
`refresh_nightwing_surfaces.py` applies the same builder functions to existing
full-pass assets while preserving their fitted head/body and rig; full builds
use the same new construction.
