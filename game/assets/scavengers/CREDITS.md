# Character asset credits

The MakeHuman **hm08 base mesh, child targets, default skeleton/weights and eye
proxy** are CC0 1.0 assets by the MakeHuman team (including Data Collection AB,
Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier). The source commit is recorded in
`game/data/scavenger_design.json`. See MAKEHUMAN-LICENSE.md in this directory.

**short02** (Kai) and the former **long01** (Camille) are MakeHuman system hair assets,
released under CC0 1.0. They were fitted to the children, recolored, and assigned
game materials.

- Source: https://www.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

**punkduck_alpha7_curly2** (Corinne and Wren) is the Alpha 7 Curly2 adaptation by
**punkduck**, distributed in the official MakeHuman hair03 collection as **CC-BY**.
It was fitted, recolored to strawberry blonde for Corinne and dark brown for
Wren, shortened to Wren's shoulder-length silhouette, and weighted between head
and upper torso. Wren's teenage body uses the pinned CC0 female child/young
MakeHuman macro targets. The original .obj/.mhclo files retain their legacy MakeClothes
AGPL3/external-tools output-license headers; the current distribution catalog's
CC-BY designation is recorded here without changing those original headers.
No endorsement by the artist is implied.

- Original asset: http://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/477
- Current distribution/license catalog: https://www.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/hair03.html

The clothing, backpacks, gathered leather pouch, runtime poses and eyelid shells
were built for this project. Downloaded source assets remain in the ignored
`cache/human-source/` directory. The generated GLB files remain ignored under the
repository's existing generated-asset policy. Use the fetch/build scripts to
recreate them; preserve these credits when distributing exported characters.

**elvs_hazel_hair** (Camille) is by **Elvaerwyn**, distributed in the official
MakeHuman hair02 collection as **CC-BY**. It is fitted to Camille and recolored
light blonde. Original files and author/license headers are preserved.

- Catalog: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/hair02.html

The UV-space lip boundary texture is a **CC0 1.0** MPFB asset from the MakeHuman
team. Its pinned source is recorded in `game/data/lip_boundary_source.json`;
`MPFB-ASSET-LICENSE.md` retains the license. The colored skin textures are generated
from that boundary and the character palettes; no skin geometry is changed.
