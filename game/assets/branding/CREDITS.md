# Startup artwork

## Wings of Fire wordmark

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Logo_Wings_of_Fire.svg
Original SVG: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7f/Logo_Wings_of_Fire.svg
Downloaded 2026-11-08. Vector upload by Mrmw, 2024-11-17, based on the series
wordmark from Scholastic. Commons identifies it as PD-textlogo (simple text/shapes)
and separately flags trademark restrictions. This is source provenance, not a
claim of endorsement or a license to the book series or its characters.

The only SVG modification is an explicit white fill, tinted gold by the splash
screen. The vector paths are retained intact. Wings of Fire is by Tui T.
Sutherland, published by Scholastic. This is an unofficial fan-made tribute.

## Clay launcher portrait

`clay-icon.png` is a 1024 × 1024 RGBA render of the project's current Clay model,
in three-quarter view with a transparent background. Rendered by
`game/tools/render_app_icon.gd` using the existing model's skin weights to isolate
his head and the game's authored procedural materials. The camera centers the
visible silhouette and zooms it to 110% of the square's height, with a small
crop at the horn/chin tips so the face fills the launcher icon.
No AI-generated asset,
stock illustration, or separate reinterpretation is used. Godot generates the
native multi-resolution `.icns` during macOS export.
